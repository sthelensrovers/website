01 About the club

St Helens Rovers was founded in 2008 as a men's football club, built on the belief that football should be competitive, enjoyable and accessible to local players.

Since then, the club has grown significantly. What started with a single men's team expanded into a successful junior section, with two teams completing the full player pathway. Building on that success, the club introduced girls' and women's football through its She Inspires programme and Women's First Team, before further expanding with new Under-7s and Under-8s teams.

Today, St Helens Rovers provides football opportunities for players across different ages and stages of the game, while remaining committed to development, enjoyment and competitive football within the local community.

We are always looking for new players, coaches and volunteers. Whether you're completely new to the game, returning to football or looking for a new club, we'd love to hear from you.

03 Our history

  1. 2008

    St Helens Rovers Football Club is formed with a Men's First Team.

  2. 2019

    Men's First Team - South Lancs Counties Unity Cup Winners, defeating Ormskirk Cavaliers 4-2.

  3. 2022

    Launch the clubs first junior section with an Under 13s Mixed Team and an Under 12 Girl's Team

  4. 2024

    Womens First Team and She Inspires launch their first campaigns

  5. Today

    The club currently runs four teams: under-7s, under-8s, She Inspires and the Women’s First Team.
