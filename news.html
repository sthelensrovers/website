Club news

Match reports, announcements and everything else happening around the club. Newest stories first.

5 October 2026

Coaches Wanted: Looking for new coaches!

We're always on the lookout for new and additional coaches and welcome interest from players past and present.

As well as general interest, our U7's team are currently looking for a coach to lead them on their journey. Email sthelensrovers@gmail.com for more information.

2 October 2026

Women’s football: She Inspires and First Team welcome new players

Our two women’s sides are always happy to see new faces, whether you are new to the game or returning to it.

She Inspires train on Tuesdays at 8pm and play on Sundays in the LCFA She Inspires League. The Women’s First Team train on Wednesdays at 8pm and play on Sundays in the Liverpool Women’s League. Email sthelensrovers@gmail.com to come along to a session.

2 October 2026

Under-7s and under-8s: Friday night training at 6pm

Our under-7s and under-8s train together every Friday night at 6pm.

Both teams play their matches on Saturdays in the Warrington Junior League. If your child would like to give it a go, email sthelensrovers@gmail.com and we will let you know how to get started.

2 October 2026

Welcome to the new club website

St Helens Rovers, formed in 2008, now has a home online.

You can find our teams, training times, links to fixtures and league tables, and how to get in touch on the home page. Club news will be posted here.
